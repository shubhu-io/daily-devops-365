# Phase 4: Authority & Network (Days 181 – 270)

> **Core Objective**: Transition from learner and builder to authoritative contributor. Demonstrate senior-level engineering judgment, production reliability principles, security governance, and open-source leadership. Build meaningful, peer-level relationships with senior staff engineers, founders, and hiring directors.

---

## Phase Overview

- **Timeline**: Months 7, 8, & 9 (Days 181 to 270)
- **Primary Pillars & Themes**:
  - **Month 7 (Days 181–210)**: **Observability, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) & Telemetry** (Prometheus PromQL, Grafana dashboard design, OpenTelemetry distributed tracing, Loki log aggregation, SLO/SLI error budget engineering, and incident on-call post-mortems).
  - **Month 8 (Days 211–240)**: **Advanced DevSecOps, Runtime Defense & Zero-Trust Architecture** (Enterprise HashiCorp Vault secret injection, Falco runtime kernel threat detection, Kyverno/OPA admission controllers, vulnerability remediation, and container hardening).
  - **Month 9 (Days 241–270)**: **Advanced Distributed Systems, eBPF, Service Meshes & Open Source Contributions** (eBPF vs iptables with Cilium, Istio mTLS service meshes, Platform Engineering Internal Developer Portals with Backstage, and contributing upstream pull requests to CNCF projects).

---

## High-Signal Networking & Authority Rules

1. **Comment for Knowledge, Not Reach**: Every single comment left on peers' and senior engineers' posts must add tangible nuance, architectural trade-offs, or production post-mortem context (using Frameworks A, B, C, or D from `00_SYSTEM_STRATEGY/03_NETWORKING_PLAYBOOK.md`).
2. **Defend Decisions with Data & Trade-Offs**: Senior engineers never say "Tool X is the best." They explain: *"We chose Tool X over Tool Y because of latency requirements, despite the operational overhead of managing its state."*
3. **Contribute Upstream**: Engage in real open-source discussions, issue triage, and documentation PRs in repositories you actively use (Terraform providers, Helm charts, Prometheus exporters).
